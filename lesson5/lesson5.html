What is deep sea exploration?

We know more about the surface of Mars than we do about the deepest parts of our own ocean. Scientists use specially built submarines, remotely operated vehicles and underwater cameras to explore a world of darkness, cold temperatures and crushing pressure.

What could go wrong?

Technology does not always work perfectly in the deep ocean. Cameras can fail, footage can be corrupted, and extreme pressure can damage equipment. When this happens, scientists may need other ways to record what they observed.

What is a scientific diagram?

A scientific diagram is a drawing of an experiment set-up. Some scientific equipment has standard symbols, creating a common visual language that scientists can interpret consistently.

Expedition field guide

Key science language

Scientific diagram

A simplified representation of an experimental set-up using clear conventions and equipment symbols.

Scientific drawing

A clear, accurate line drawing used to record an observation.

Fair test

An investigation where the variable being tested is changed while relevant conditions are kept the same.

Observation

Information collected by carefully noticing and recording features or changes.
