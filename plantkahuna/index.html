GTAC • Plant Lab

Plan a plant experiment.

Plants need many things to stay alive and grow well, like light, water and food. In this experiment you will change one thing. Then you will see how it changes the plant's growth and health.

What you changeIndependent variable
What you measureDependent variable
What you keep the sameControlled variables

How do we measure growth? We measure how tall the plant is and how many leaves it has. How do we measure health? We look at the leaves to see how well the plant is doing.

Which plant will you grow? Pea plants are just one example. Choose any plant you like.
Where will you grow your plants? Pots, soil and water. Light comes from a window or a cupboard.
